How the complaint portal works

Submit without creating an account. Keep both your reference and private tracking secret safe.

1. Submit

Choose anonymous or identified contact, select a category, describe the issue and its village/ward and landmark, and optionally attach a photo.

2. Keep the receipt

The portal creates a reference and one-time private tracking secret. The secret lets you view the timeline, send feedback, or request reinspection.

3. Follow updates

Use the reference for basic status. Use both reference and secret for the detailed action history and resolution evidence.

4. Review evidence

Staff must provide a work summary and resolution photo before marking a complaint resolved. Citizens can request reinspection within seven days.
